📋 Citizen Reporting Guidelines

Your submission directly triggers municipal emergency response and CENRO field inspections under Republic Act 9003 (Ecological Solid Waste Management) and Clean Water Act (RA 9275).

  • Take clear photos: Capture both close-up evidence and surrounding context.
  • Precise location: Include recognizable street corners or barangay halls.
  • Emergency Hazards: For immediate chemical spills or wildfire threats, call 911 immediately in addition to this digital report.

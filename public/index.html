🏛️ Municipal Reporting Protocols

Reports undergo automated triage and are forwarded to the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO). Critical issues receive on-site dispatch within 4 hours.

⚖️ Republic Act 9003 & 8749 Compliance:
Citizens reporting verified violations are protected under Environmental Whistleblower regulations. Falsification of reports is strictly prohibited.
